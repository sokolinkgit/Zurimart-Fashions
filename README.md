> *With a dedicated scalable collection for Men*

## 🌟 Key Highlights

- **Store Name:** **ZURIMART FASHIONS**
- **Core Focus:** Ladies Fashion (Chic Dresses, Ankara / African Fusion, Power Suits, Coords, Jumpsuits & Weekend Casuals)
- **Scalable Men’s Section:** Expanding line of Men’s Resort Shirts, African Craft Wear & Smart-Casual Blazers
- **Delivery:** **Countrywide Delivery across all 47 Counties in Kenya** (Same-Day in Nairobi & Murang’a Town; 24h via G4S, Wells Fargo, Easy Coach & 2NK Sacco)
- **Physical Shop Locations:**
  1. **Shop 1: Kawangware Branch (Nairobi)** – Naivasha Road, Stage 46 / BP Petrol Station, Dagoretti North
  2. **Shop 2: Murang’a Town Branch (Central)** – Uhuru Highway CBD, Opposite Murang’a Municipal Market
- **Official WhatsApp / Contact for Orders:** **`0724293125`** (`+254 724 293 125`)
- **Authentic Representation:** All ladies photographed in the Ladies Section are **Kenyan [Black ladies]** in authentic Kenyan urban and boutique settings.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Interactive Department Switching:** Seamless toggle between Ladies Fashion (Main Boutique) and Men’s Collection (Scalable).
- **Comprehensive Category & Occasion Filtering:** Filter by Dresses & Gowns, Ankara Fusion, Workwear, Weekend Chic, Church / Sunday Best, and Night Out.
- **Physical Store Availability Indicators:** See real-time stock availability for Kawangware vs Murang’a branches on every product.
- **1-Click WhatsApp Ordering:** Every product card and quick-view modal features a pre-formatted WhatsApp order button linking to `0724293125`.
- **Interactive Countrywide Shipping Rate Calculator:** Select any of Kenya's 47 counties to view estimated delivery fees and courier timelines.
- **Full Shopping Bag Drawer:** Add multiple items with size & color choices, calculate delivery, and checkout directly via WhatsApp with an itemized receipt.
- **Floating WhatsApp Concierge:** Fast customer support popup with direct options to ask about Kawangware directions, Murang’a directions, or custom orders.
